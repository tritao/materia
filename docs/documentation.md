you want to do; each links into the others.

## By what you want to do

- **Build a machine in code.** [The tutorial](tutorials/gantry-picker.md), then
  [assemblies and mates](concepts/modelling/assemblies-and-mates.md),
  [machine components](concepts/modelling/machine-components.md) and
  [transmissions](concepts/modelling/transmissions.md). The
  [component catalogue](/components/) shows every part you can use.
- **Make it move and do work.**
  [Execution plans](concepts/motion/execution-plans.md),
  [toolpaths](concepts/process/toolpaths.md) and
  [manufacturing outputs](concepts/process/manufacturing-outputs.md).
- **Simulate it and run it on hardware.**
  [Simulation worlds](concepts/execution/simulation-worlds.md), the
  [robot runtime](concepts/execution/robot-runtime.md) and
  [the controller boundary](concepts/execution/controller-boundary.md).
- **Use the editor.** The [editor manual](manual/README.md), and the
  [gallery](product/gallery.md) of machines to open in it.
- **Contribute.**
  [Contributing](https://github.com/tritao/materia/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md),
  the [roadmap](product/roadmap.md), and how these pages are written and
  checked: the [documentation plan](DOCS_PLAN.md).
