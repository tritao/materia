# Hobber frames, drives and feedback: plan

Started 2026-11-03 from a review of the drive frames and weldments. Dimensions in mm.

## Why

A review of `HobberSaddle` and `FlexsplineHobber` found:

- The Y drive frame (`GE-Y-drive`) hangs on one 20 x 20 block resting on the swivel plate's top edge; the
  same block rests on the moving Y saddle.
- Moving parts sit flat on fixed ones with zero clearance: the X nut bracket bottom (z 155) on the X drive
  deck top, and the Y nut bracket on the Y drive plate (x 45) and the swivel plate top (z 95).
- The Y nut bracket hits the BF12 housing at Y = +10 (about 2 mm) and +15 (about 7 mm) after the 170 mm Y
  screw: `HobberYLayout` measured to the nut flange, not to the 17.5 mm bracket behind it.
- The rear spindle cradle and the Y nut bracket are joined by small face contacts; motor plates are 5 mm with
  only a shaft hole (no pilot bore, no bolt holes).
- `HobberInterferenceChecks` flags only overlap volume, so face contacts and tiny joins pass, and it already
  reports 412 known overlaps that hide new ones.
- The scales are exposed tapes sized to the screw length, with no reading head.

## Decisions

- **Construction:** bolted and dowel-pinned parts, no welding. Ground plate where a face only needs to be
  flat, ground flat bar for blocks and spacers, laser-cut plate for seats and covers. CNC milling from a
  machine shop (China) is acceptable where one milled part replaces several bolted plates or gives a precise
  face more simply. Rules for milled parts: within about 600 mm, at most two setups on a 3-axis mill, tight
  tolerances only on functional faces, no weld-then-machine.
- **Screw drives (X, Y, Z):** SFU1605, catalogue BK12 motor bracket (HZMotion M60D-BK12-C type) with BF12 and a
  Leadshine 400 W servo, braked on Z (`SupportedScrewDrive.m60dBk12()` parts). Screw supports aligned to the nut at
  assembly, then pinned; their seats need drilled and tapped holes only. Z falls back to BK15 + SFU2005 if the
  400 W sizing check fails.
- **Nuts:** a simple steel nut block (flat bar or small milled block) per axis. No DSG housings (usually
  aluminium, 20 mm centre height against the BK12's 25).
- **A axis:** ZeroErr eRob110I V6, revision D (MC2E.DX), EtherCAT, ratio 100, 20-bit output encoder
  (±7/±15″), brake, 48 V bus. CAD: `build/suppliers/zeroerr/` (git-ignored).
  Dimensions measured on the maker's STEP (2026-11-04), axis along its Y: output face at +38.0, housing front face (16 M4 holes) 16.0 behind it,
  the Ø110 body ends 76.2 behind the output face, the Ø85 rear boss 80.2, the connectors 83.6 (the earlier 6 and 2 mm estimates were wrong).
  The housing enters its host from the rear into a pocket (8 deep) behind a 5 mm web with the output bore.
- **Scales:** Yuheng JFT-40 (slim, sealed, absolute, BiSS-C): X ML 120 ±3 µm, Z ML 220 ±5 µm (H4: 200 stroke + 2 × 5 overtravel; the RFQ asked 270), Y ML 70 ±5 µm,
  read by the controller. Datasheets: `build/suppliers/yuheng/` (git-ignored).
- **Spindle:** bought RobotDigg 2049 D80 BT30 belt-drive cartridge in a milled split clamp, with a separate made BT30 hob arbor. The P1 evidence and assumptions below supersede the R0 custom-cartridge choice; P2 supplies the matched-pair floating outboard housing below.
- **B drive (head redesign, 2026-11-04):** the servo no longer hangs behind the cartridge on a bell adapter. It stands beside the cartridge, above it,
  shaft toward the rear, its flange on one milled **cradle** (`GE-motor-cradle`: floor 12 (8 ahead of y 195, an 18 foot under the wall resting on a tongue of the seat block) on the spindle seat block's top, 8 wall with vertical slots for the servo flange, a jack lug, one windowed 6 rib; 8 mm toward -x of the cartridge's vertical so it clears the Y drive) and drives
  the cartridge's rear stub through an HTD5M 15 mm belt, **40T on the servo and on the spindle** (ratio 1:1, decided 2026-11-04: the 1.25 reduction made the CAM reference cut, hob Ø32 at 300 m/min = 2984 rpm, need 3730 motor rpm, above the ELM2H-1000's 3000 rpm rated where torque falls; direct the reference cut runs at 99.5 % of rated speed and the hob gets 3.18 N m rated / 9.5 peak, against an assumed 1.65 N m cutting bound). 125 mm centres (catalogue 450-5M belt, 90 teeth), tension set by a jack screw: the servo slides in vertical slots in the cradle wall and is clamped by its four M6 once the belt rings at the pretension. **The B master encoder is
  the cartridge's own 19-bit absolute encoder** (`addEncoder("hob-angle", "B", ...)`, on the housing rear face with the rotor on the stub; ring-encoder envelope Ø80 x 14, assumed, no catalogue row);
  the servo keeps its own for the drive's loop (`hob-motor`, feedback of `hob-drive` on the `motor-turn` joint). Belt wind-up and pulley error are now inside the gearbox loop's measurement, not
  hidden behind it. **Cartridge acceptance requirement:** a belt-drive cartridge rated for the belt's pull, 450 N on the pulley (pretension 225 N per strand, slack side kept at a quarter of the 300 N peak force, assumed rule), on the assumed rear stub (the internal bearing location is unpublished); ask for at least 700 N radial; a motor spindle (same seat, same encoder interface) is the upgrade path. Pulley and belt rows are the library's generic `TimingPulley`/`TimingBelt`
  (no vendor catalogue rows yet): pulleys, belt and encoder are to be replaced by catalogue parts when the cartridge maker names them.
- **Y:** two rails + SFU1605 screw with the standard bracket and a nut block (R0). Bought KK modules rejected: the
  shortest KK86 is 440 mm long (210 stroke for a 30 mm need) on a 230 mm head, +5.7 kg on the eRob, stiffness
  unpublished; KK60 cannot take the 400 W servo.
- **X carriage:** bought ground 20 mm deck with separate bolted bright-bar ribs and four full-footprint sole pads (XC); X rail height unchanged. Bond is optional and gets no stiffness credit.
- **Machined parts:** a part exists either as a structural member or as the deliberate adjustment/interface element at a joint (height
  spacer, alignment bar, actuator adapter). Don't merge parts to reduce count; judge by the joints in the precision paths (tool-to-work
  stiffness, screw alignment, scale alignment). Kept separate on purpose: the scale seat bars and the Z scale arm (alignment and shimming
  of housing and head), the two Y brackets (the nut seat travels between them), the Y nut seat bar (the nut's height spacer on a 12 mm
  milled edge), the eRob adapter ring (isolates the actuator's bolt pattern; a Honpine fallback changes only the ring). X and Z have one
  ground pad bar under both screw supports, so both seats are coplanar by grinding (one interface fewer in the screw-alignment path).
- **Swivel plate:** ground *steel* plate (15 mm), not aluminium. It sits in the tool-to-work path, carrying the steel Y rails and the spindle seat:
  aluminium is about a third as stiff, and its thermal expansion against the steel rails bows the plate. The model already used steel
  for its mass; only the stock row said aluminium, so the A-axis head load does not change.
- **Ground stock tolerances** are those of the stock as bought (thickness about +-0.03 to 0.1 mm, flatness 0.05 to 0.1 mm per 100 mm;
  assumed typical values, each row names its source: DIN 59350-type flat steel, Blanchard-ground steel plate, MIC-6 type tooling plate). A
  functional face tighter than that is *ground after cutting* and listed so on the part's route.

- **Crash protection (decided by the lead 2026-11-05):** a jam (stop hit, collision, stuck axis) must not load the structure with the motor's peak thrust (4.3 kN). Two measures, both declared:
  (1) *Limit*: each linear drive is set to a torque limit (`CrashProtection`, `HobberCrashProtection`): the largest multiple of 5 % of the motor's rated torque that keeps the
  thrust at or under 2 x the axis sizing thrust (and not under 1.5 x, so it never clips the sizing duty): X 65 % of rated = 0.826 N m = 934 N (1.95 x the sizing 478 N), Y 65 % = 0.826 N m = 934 N (2.00 x 468 N),
  Z 125 % = 1.588 N m = 1795 N (1.98 x 905 N), against 4309 N at the motor's 3.82 N m peak; plus a following-error trip of 1 mm (ASSUMED, jam energy under 2 J; it must be set above the tuned axis's
  largest following error at rapid speed). **To be set in the three drives (Leadshine EL8)**: the internal torque limit (share of rated torque, both directions) and the excessive-position-error alarm; per
  the EL8-EC manual (V2.0.1): **P00.13** "1st Torque Limit" (index 2013h, % of rated motor current, range 0–750, factory default 500 %): set X 65, Y 65, Z 125; the drive
  applies the smaller of P00.13 and EtherCAT 6072h (max torque, 0.1 %), and 60E0h/60E1h (positive/negative torque limit, 0.1 %) may set it per direction from the controller.
  **P00.14** "Excessive Position Deviation" (index 2014h, unit 0.1 rev, factory default 30 = 3 rev = 15 mm on the 5 mm lead, alarm Er180): set 2 (0.2 rev = 1 mm) once tuning
  confirms the largest following error at rapid stays below it; over EtherCAT the following-error window is 6065h with time-out 6066h (default 10 ms). Write all at commissioning
  and read them back. The model has no drive parameter store (`MotorDriver` has a current rating and the
  actuator record the motor's peak effort), so the settings live in `HobberCrashProtection` and `HobberJointLoads` takes the momentary loads from them (limited thrust plus the weight along the screw: Z 494 N, Y 69 N).
  (2) *Locate*: the nut blocks are located against the thrust by dowels or a key, not by screw friction. X and Z: two Ø6 H7 dowels in the block's base end (`NutBlock.pinned`, 10 mm either side of the axis,
  7 mm deep) and in the carriage deck / Z saddle plate (8 mm), derived joints (`PinnedFace`). Y: no room for dowels (the base screws are 16 mm apart on a 20 mm base, a dowel would leave 1.7 mm of web),
  so the seat has a key: two ribs 3.5 x 3 mm, 42 long, either side of the block's base (`LocatingKey`, milled from 19 mm bar), counted by `JointSlip` as the lesser of bearing and root shear. The Y seat is held
  to the saddle by its two M4 (friction only, x1.9 on the limited jam; pinning the 12 mm edge would need Ø4 pins at 4 mm from its sides).

- **P1 BT30 purchase and evidence (2026-11-05):** use RobotDigg product 2049 option A, D80 BT30, 6000 rpm, with HTD5M and the stated 7008+7006 bearing family ([listing](https://www.robotdigg.com/product/2049)). Its linked drawing says **NT30** and 7007/7006; do not use it as verified BT30 geometry. MAS BT30 shank data comes from [PTP CNC’s shank drawing/table](https://www.ptpcnctoolings.com/shanks/bt): gauge Ø31.75, 7/24, length 48.4, flange Ø46 x 20 after a 2 mm gap, drive slots 16.1 wide with floors 16.3 from axis, M12 stud. Bought spindle dog projection is **ASSUMED** 10 mm and matched by the holder slots. Keep a deliberate 4000 rpm operating cap and the 40T/40T belt (the supplied 36/45T pulley is replaced). Unpublished packaging is **ASSUMED**: rear-to-gauge 180, Ø110 front register at 170..180, clamp land 10..168, Ø20 x 36 rear stub, encoder and mass partition. The clamp must stop before the register; these envelopes need the chosen option's outline before manufacture. Buy the complete cartridge once; its internal bearings, shaft, gland and drawbar are not separate made parts. Keep its rotor as an included envelope for B kinematics. The BT30 interface is taper-located with a pull stud, not dual face contact. Hob location remains the plain Ø16 seat, driven by an integral end-face key (5 x 3 radial x 2 axial; tool slot 5.2 x 2.2 **ASSUMED**) and clamped by an M16x1 nut (24 AF x 8, design inputs; 2000 N assembly preload assumed, secured with medium-strength thread-locking compound and witness-marked). P1 used the Ø15 x 22 journal; P2 extends it to 29 mm and adds a proper inner-ring abutment. RobotDigg publishes no runout or belt-load rating: the existing 2 µm model errors and ≥700 N belt-load requirement remain **ASSUMED acceptance requirements**, not catalogue claims. [Haozhi DGZZ-10020](https://haozhihs.net/show.php?id=237) is a different Ø100 spindle (≤3 µm nose, ≤2 µm taper, G0.4); its ratings are not transferred to the D80 purchase. The front register requires a Ø116 relief through the Y saddle with at least 2 mm at the reader spine and forward withdrawal; the outboard carrier is a separate structural service interface (4 derived M4 with washers and 2 derived Ø4 H7 pins, with a declared slip load), removed with the support and Y reading head before cartridge withdrawal. A 2.3 mm spine carries the reader behind the clearance channel (the extra 1 mm relief in the adjacent wall clears the reader screw heads); its clamping screws bear on that spine, not in an empty former counterbore. Long-term choice: a standard replaceable cartridge and standard taper, with a separate made arbor, rather than preserving the custom TF20 internals.

- **P2 outboard support (2026-11-05):** two SKF 7002 CDGA/P4A universally matchable bearings, DB, in a ground D40 sliding sleeve. [SKF's public product sheet](https://docs.rs-online.com/4857/A700000013821392.pdf) states 15 x 32 x 9 mm, 15 degrees, C 5.2 kN / C0 2.45 kN, 0.03 kg each, class-A 20 N unmounted preload and 17 N/um axial stiffness for a DB/DF pair. These are catalogue facts; installed radial stiffness, runout and the thermal case remain project assumptions. Clamp the outer-ring stack between sleeve shoulder and M36x1 gland; clamp the inner-ring stack between arbor D20 shoulder and ground spacer/M15x1 nut. The matched bearing faces establish preload, not a guessed nut torque. Arbor 180 long: D20 shoulder 146..151 (fillet <=0.2), D15 journal 151..180, pair 151..169, spacer 169..174, retaining thread 174..180. The sleeve floats +/-1 mm between fixed housing and cap stops: thermal growth 0.0648 mm at assumed 30 K and 12e-6/K, plus assumed assembly allowance 0.2 mm, leaves 0.7352 mm margin. The cartridge remains the only axial locator. Housing screws and washers recess 5.5 into the carrier (0.5 below the Y saddle face), so the housing is secured before fitting the carrier. Fit the Y reading head before the whole outboard group. Indicate the housing, torque and refit on carrier pins, then ream/pin its two D4 H7 locations; actual selected pins engage 4 mm each side. Radial worst-case budget 5 um = 2 indication + 1 seats + 1.5 bearing + 0.5 thermal, all assumed acceptance requirements (2 um radial indication means 4 um TIR). Qualify the shaft fit, retained preload and thread-locking torque at assembly. Nut/spacer leave first, then the complete support, then the cartridge; declared sweeps prove those paths. The made housing, sleeve, gland, cap and spacer are deliberate alignment, thermal and retention interfaces. Steel is fixed in their recipes so the thermal design cannot silently become aluminium.

- **P3 motor position (2026-11-05): retain the top-mounted drive.** Inward beside placement collides with the Z saddle (71800.529 mm3 in the 24-state structural study). Outward placement clears those sampled members (11.268 mm minimum versus top 7 mm), but an assumed 0.503 kg interface blank gives estimated head mass 38.48 -> 38.98 kg and eRob tilt 47.58 -> 52.82 N m. A holding demand improves only 34.06 -> 33.62 N m. Modes have no demonstrated improvement: rigid-interface same-cradle bound 1432 Hz; assumed one-third adapter participation gives 1398 Hz, with extra joint stiffness unqualified. Candidate fasteners are not invented or added to the BOM; a real joint design would be needed before any adoption. The study remains validation code, with one installed layout. Current head-demand reporting subtracts the actual posed output face, not static X124; the corrected 47.58 N m supersedes older tilt numbers, whose geometry did not change. The world-translation regression check guards that distinction.

- **P4 fixed chip shields (2026-11-05):** adopt option (b): four fixed X side sheets, front/rear lids, a moving X skirt and two Z shields. Eight roles are single-blank 2 mm steel, R2 inside bends; the rear lid is flat cut plate. `FoldedSheet` records thickness, radius and bend count separately from machining setups. All fixing holes/hardware derive from joints and carry declared slip loads (own weight + 0.5 m/s2 and **ASSUMED** 2 N debris load); no welded corners, hand fastener list or casting change. The X ground pad becomes standard 100 x 15 stock on the existing 100 mm insert to give guard threads proper edge distance; support-seat footprints remain separate from the guard lands. Z nut-block heads are recessed 6.5 mm in the existing front-face setup. Fixed lids are bench assembled; four capped D10 tool ports reach the foot screws; plug dimensions and NBR oil compatibility are **ASSUMED procurement requirements**. Moving guards bolt to the carriage underside, preserving its two setups. X end overlaps **14.5 / 39.5 mm** at 5 mm linear overtravel, side flat overlap **6 mm**; Z shields extend Z282..750. The geometry study passed **24 states**, minimum moving gap **2.794 mm**; **1098 actual-solid ingress samples** pass. Nine shields weigh **4.583 kg**, of which **2.906 kg** moves with X; B head unchanged. Existing wipers remain and the enclosure contains mist. No channel drain is declared: no fall or bore exists. The made package includes the formed STEP and bend specification; the sheet shop must develop its flat pattern with its tooling/bend allowance.

- **P5 service architecture (2026-11-05):** use the physical X nut-block front face **232.5 mm** as the layout datum, with block to 248.5 unchanged; nut origin **213.5**, local axis +X, flange motorward. X drive station is the actual nut origin; no former-orientation or upward-removal code path remains.
