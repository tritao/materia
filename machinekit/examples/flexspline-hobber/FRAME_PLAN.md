# Hobber frames, drives and feedback: plan

Started 2026-11-03 from a review of the drive frames and weldments. Dimensions in mm.

## Why

A review of `HobberSaddle` and `FlexsplineHobber` found:

- The Y drive frame (`GE-Y-drive`) hangs on one 20 x 20 block resting on the swivel plate's top edge; the
  same block rests on the moving Y saddle.
- Moving parts sit flat on fixed ones with zero clearance: the X nut bracket bottom (z 155) on the X drive
  deck top, and the Y nut bracket on the Y drive plate (x 45) and the swivel plate top (z 95).
- The Y nut bracket hits the BF12 housing at Y = +10 (about 2 mm) and +15 (about 7 mm) after the 170 mm Y
  screw: `HobberYLayout` measured to the nut flange, not to the 17.5 mm bracket behind it.
- The rear spindle cradle and the Y nut bracket are joined by small face contacts; motor plates are 5 mm with
  only a shaft hole (no pilot bore, no bolt holes).
- `HobberInterferenceChecks` flags only overlap volume, so face contacts and tiny joins pass, and it already
  reports 412 known overlaps that hide new ones.
- The scales are exposed tapes sized to the screw length, with no reading head.

## Decisions

- **Construction:** bolted and dowel-pinned parts, no welding. Ground plate where a face only needs to be
  flat, ground flat bar for blocks and spacers, laser-cut plate for seats and covers. CNC milling from a
  machine shop (China) is acceptable where one milled part replaces several bolted plates or gives a precise
  face more simply. Rules for milled parts: within about 600 mm, at most two setups on a 3-axis mill, tight
  tolerances only on functional faces, no weld-then-machine.
- **Screw drives (X, Y, Z):** SFU1605, catalogue BK12 motor bracket (HZMotion M60D-BK12-C type) with BF12 and a
  Leadshine 400 W servo, braked on Z (`SupportedScrewDrive.m60dBk12()` parts). Screw supports aligned to the nut at
  assembly, then pinned; their seats need drilled and tapped holes only. Z falls back to BK15 + SFU2005 if the
  400 W sizing check fails.
- **Nuts:** a simple steel nut block (flat bar or small milled block) per axis. No DSG housings (usually
  aluminium, 20 mm centre height against the BK12's 25).
- **A axis:** ZeroErr eRob110I V6, revision D (MC2E.DX), EtherCAT, ratio 100, 20-bit output encoder
  (±7/±15″), brake, 48 V bus. CAD: `build/suppliers/zeroerr/` (git-ignored).
  Dimensions measured on the maker's STEP (2026-11-04), axis along its Y: output face at +38.0, housing front face (16 M4 holes) 16.0 behind it,
  the Ø110 body ends 76.2 behind the output face, the Ø85 rear boss 80.2, the connectors 83.6 (the earlier 6 and 2 mm estimates were wrong).
  The housing enters its host from the rear into a pocket (8 deep) behind a 5 mm web with the output bore.
- **Scales:** Yuheng JFT-40 (slim, sealed, absolute, BiSS-C): X ML 120 ±3 µm, Z ML 220 ±5 µm (H4: 200 stroke + 2 × 5 overtravel; the RFQ asked 270), Y ML 70 ±5 µm,
  read by the controller. Datasheets: `build/suppliers/yuheng/` (git-ignored).
- **Spindle:** bought Ø80 g6 cartridge with the maker's outboard support. No standard flange-mount Ø80 cartridge was
  found (R0), so the seat is one milled split-clamp block for the Ø80 housing; ask the spindle maker for a flange anyway.
- **B drive (head redesign, 2026-11-04):** the servo no longer hangs behind the cartridge on a bell adapter. It stands beside the cartridge, above it,
  shaft toward the rear, its flange on one milled **cradle** (`GE-motor-cradle`: floor 12 (8 ahead of y 195, an 18 foot under the wall resting on a tongue of the seat block) on the spindle seat block's top, 8 wall with vertical slots for the servo flange, a jack lug, one windowed 6 rib; 8 mm toward -x of the cartridge's vertical so it clears the Y drive) and drives
  the cartridge's rear stub through an HTD5M 15 mm belt, **40T on the servo and on the spindle** (ratio 1:1, decided 2026-11-04: the 1.25 reduction made the CAM reference cut, hob Ø32 at 300 m/min = 2984 rpm, need 3730 motor rpm, above the ELM2H-1000's 3000 rpm rated where torque falls; direct the reference cut runs at 99.5 % of rated speed and the hob gets 3.18 N m rated / 9.5 peak, against an assumed 1.65 N m cutting bound). 125 mm centres (catalogue 450-5M belt, 90 teeth), tension set by a jack screw: the servo slides in vertical slots in the cradle wall and is clamped by its four M6 once the belt rings at the pretension. **The B master encoder is
  the cartridge's own 19-bit absolute encoder** (`addEncoder("hob-angle", "B", ...)`, on the housing rear face with the rotor on the stub; ring-encoder envelope Ø80 x 14, assumed, no catalogue row);
  the servo keeps its own for the drive's loop (`hob-motor`, feedback of `hob-drive` on the `motor-turn` joint). Belt wind-up and pulley error are now inside the gearbox loop's measurement, not
  hidden behind it. **Spec for ZYS:** a belt-drive cartridge rated for the belt's pull, 450 N on the pulley (pretension 225 N per strand, slack side kept at a quarter of the 300 N peak force, assumed rule), 32.5 mm behind the
  6205 tail bearing; ask for at least 700 N radial; a motor spindle (same seat, same encoder interface) is the upgrade path. Pulley and belt rows are the library's generic `TimingPulley`/`TimingBelt`
  (no vendor catalogue rows yet): pulleys, belt and encoder are to be replaced by catalogue parts when the cartridge maker names them.
- **Y:** two rails + SFU1605 screw with the standard bracket and a nut block (R0). Bought KK modules rejected: the
  shortest KK86 is 440 mm long (210 stroke for a 30 mm need) on a 230 mm head, +5.7 kg on the eRob, stiffness
  unpublished; KK60 cannot take the 400 W servo.
- **X carriage:** one milled steel part, 20 mm deck with 25 mm ribs, U open at the front (H1: a front bar meets the Y
  guides at A ±15°; a thicker deck needs the X rails lower).
- **Machined parts:** a part exists either as a structural member or as the deliberate adjustment/interface element at a joint (height
  spacer, alignment bar, actuator adapter). Don't merge parts to reduce count; judge by the joints in the precision paths (tool-to-work
  stiffness, screw alignment, scale alignment). Kept separate on purpose: the scale seat bars and the Z scale arm (alignment and shimming
  of housing and head), the two Y brackets (the nut seat travels between them), the Y nut seat bar (the nut's height spacer on a 12 mm
  milled edge), the eRob adapter ring (isolates the actuator's bolt pattern; a Honpine fallback changes only the ring). X and Z have one
  ground pad bar under both screw supports, so both seats are coplanar by grinding (one interface fewer in the screw-alignment path).
- **Swivel plate:** ground *steel* plate (15 mm), not aluminium. It sits in the tool-to-work path, carrying the steel Y rails and the spindle seat:
  aluminium is about a third as stiff, and its thermal expansion against the steel rails bows the plate. The model already used steel
  for its mass; only the stock row said aluminium, so the A-axis head load does not change.
- **Ground stock tolerances** are those of the stock as bought (thickness about +-0.03 to 0.1 mm, flatness 0.05 to 0.1 mm per 100 mm;
  assumed typical values, each row names its source: DIN 59350-type flat steel, Blanchard-ground steel plate, MIC-6 type tooling plate). A
  functional face tighter than that is *ground after cutting* and listed so on the part's route.

- **Crash protection (decided by the lead 2026-11-05):** a jam (stop hit, collision, stuck axis) must not load the structure with the motor's peak thrust (4.3 kN). Two measures, both declared:
  (1) *Limit*: each linear drive is set to a torque limit (`CrashProtection`, `HobberCrashProtection`): the largest multiple of 5 % of the motor's rated torque that keeps the
  thrust at or under 2 x the axis sizing thrust (and not under 1.5 x, so it never clips the sizing duty): X 65 % of rated = 0.826 N m = 934 N (1.96 x the sizing 477 N), Y 60 % = 0.762 N m = 862 N (1.86 x 462 N),
  Z 120 % = 1.524 N m = 1724 N (1.94 x 887 N), against 4309 N at the motor's 3.82 N m peak; plus a following-error trip of 1 mm (ASSUMED, jam energy under 2 J; it must be set above the tuned axis's
  largest following error at rapid speed). **To be set in the three drives (Leadshine EL8)**: the internal torque limit (share of rated torque, both directions) and the excessive-position-error alarm; per
  the EL8-EC manual (V2.0.1): **P00.13** "1st Torque Limit" (index 2013h, % of rated motor current, range 0–750, factory default 500 %): set X 65, Y 60, Z 120; the drive
  applies the smaller of P00.13 and EtherCAT 6072h (max torque, 0.1 %), and 60E0h/60E1h (positive/negative torque limit, 0.1 %) may set it per direction from the controller.
  **P00.14** "Excessive Position Deviation" (index 2014h, unit 0.1 rev, factory default 30 = 3 rev = 15 mm on the 5 mm lead, alarm Er180): set 2 (0.2 rev = 1 mm) once tuning
  confirms the largest following error at rapid stays below it; over EtherCAT the following-error window is 6065h with time-out 6066h (default 10 ms). Write all at commissioning
  and read them back. The model has no drive parameter store (`MotorDriver` has a current rating and the
  actuator record the motor's peak effort), so the settings live in `HobberCrashProtection` and `HobberJointLoads` takes the momentary loads from them (limited thrust plus the weight along the screw: Z 477 N, Y 65 N).
  (2) *Locate*: the nut blocks are located against the thrust by dowels or a key, not by screw friction. X and Z: two Ø6 H7 dowels in the block's base end (`NutBlock.pinned`, 10 mm either side of the axis,
  7 mm deep) and in the carriage deck / Z saddle plate (8 mm), derived joints (`PinnedFace`). Y: no room for dowels (the base screws are 16 mm apart on a 20 mm base, a dowel would leave 1.7 mm of web),
  so the seat has a key: two ribs 3.5 x 3 mm, 42 long, either side of the block's base (`LocatingKey`, milled from 19 mm bar), counted by `JointSlip` as the lesser of bearing and root shear. The Y seat is held
  to the saddle by its two M4 (friction only, x1.9 on the limited jam; pinning the 12 mm edge would need Ø4 pins at 4 mm from its sides).

## Open

- **Y screw centred between the rails in a 30 mm swivel plate (head redesign item 1): NOT DONE, numbers in the Log (2026-11-04).** It needs a small-screw drive family (SFU1204 or compact nut with BK10/BF10 supports and a 40-60 frame motor mount) that the library does not have; the SFU1605/BK12/M60D drive cannot be centred in a 30 mm plate (the motor mount stands 22 mm into the plate from the rail plane and its body meets the Y saddle) and
  its supports cannot sit "outside the saddle's travel" on a plate shorter than the saddle (plate 230, saddle -130..222 mm, so about 400 mm). Decision needed: new drive family rows (maker data to source) or a different Y drive.
- Spindle drive: belt strength (HTD5M 15 mm tight-side 375 N at the motor's peak torque) and the pulley/encoder parts are not checked against a maker's data; the first-mode estimate (`HobberFrameChecks.spindleDrive`) is analytic with stated joint assumptions, to be confirmed by an FE model of the cradle on the seat and an impact test.

- eRob output bearing moment rating and brake holding torque (asked from ZeroErr): R3 shows 21.9 N m tilting and 28.4 N m about A against an assumed 87 N m brake.
- Servo voltage class: the ELM2H is a 220 V motor, the rack is 400 V; the model uses an assumed winding (R4).
- **Z screw offset (decided by the lead 2026-11-04, derived):** the eRob housing stands behind the Z saddle at y 0 where the Z screw used to be; the screw keeps off it by the derived least |y| (`HobberZOffset`, y +81 on the side of the Z moving mass's centre of mass, set by the BF12 support's 2 mm gap to the housing; the casting cover allows at most 82.5). The yaw moment on the Z blocks is checked in `HobberFrameChecks.zOffsetMoments`. Open: the Z scale moved to the -y channel wall; a centred screw would need the housing moved.
- **Chip protection and service (2026-11-05, branch hobber-covers):** wipers: every guide block carries an end scraper at each end (`WayWiperCatalog`: `WIPER-HG25-SCRAPER` x 8 on the four X blocks, `WIPER-HG15-SCRAPER` x 16 on the four Z and four Y blocks; added lengths ASSUMED), as BOM lines. Way covers (`machinekit.cover`, lamella bellows box covers) do not fit as built, measured on the posed machine by `HobberFrameChecks.coverFit` (end walls and carriers 14 mm, 2 mm gaps, opening 40 x 50 round the bare screw, X stroke 64.8 mm, Z 200 mm): **X front** (BF12 to nut) has 28.7 mm free against 22 mm needed (2 folds, flange 106 of the 110 mm channel), so it fits dimensionally but has no host for its fixed wall (the pad bar's nut groove starts 2 mm behind the BF12, the insert is 100 wide, the casting wall is the 12.5 mm strip) and was not built; **X rear** (nut to BK12) has -9 mm (the nut at the far stop is 9 mm short of where the cover's carrier would stand, 31 mm short of the 22 mm fold): 31 mm more screw behind the nut (motor, BK12, bed 40 mm longer); **Z bottom** 21.5 mm and **Z top** 23 mm free against 38 mm (4 folds): 16.5 and 15 mm more screw at each end (the column 20 mm taller); **Y**: the 28 mm between the Y saddle's plate and the swivel plate holds the rails (15) and blocks and the screw, so a skin has no room (stroke 20 mm, bellows 22 mm folded plus plates): wipers only. No channel floor fall or drain was cut: the drive-seat insert (100 of the 110 mm channel) is the floor, ground flat for the pad bar, so the casting floor is two 5 mm strips; gutters need a channel at least 150 wide (22 mm each side), whose deck ribs (inner faces at y +-55) would all move. Service removals are declared and swept (`FlexsplineHobber.serviceRemovals`, `HobberFrameChecks.service`): the three screw servos and the B servo come off along their own axes, the B belt off along its pulleys' axis, the B cartridge out of its clamp rearward with the hob (X at retract; the belt off), the X screw and nut up out of the channel after sliding 25 mm back, with the carriage stack lifted off first. The paths that do not work are `serviceTries` and reported with their numbers; the eRob has no path (see Log).
- **A brake holding torque is assumed** (87 N m = rated output torque, `FlexsplineHobber.A_BRAKE_HOLDING`), as is the A acceleration limit (pi/2 rad/s^2, `A_ACCELERATION`); bearing moment rating still unknown (R3 reports the demand: see Log).
- Thicker X carriage deck: needs the X rails 10-20 mm lower on the bed inserts (user decision; today 20 mm + ribs).

- **Accuracy target (2026-11-06, user decision to confirm):** the docs state ISO 1328 class 5-6 on every parameter (PLAN.md HB12), no single class. `HobberAccuracyChecks` gates on **class 6** for fp, Fp, Fr, F-alpha, F-beta (ISO 1328-1:2013 formulas extrapolated to m 0.3); decide whether class 5 is the commitment. The gate currently **fails on Fp** (predicted class 9) because the purchased table's once-per-turn speed ripple is an assumed 0.1 % with no vendor or bench value: decide who measures it (bench at 10-20 rpm) or whether to ask the table vendor for the figure. Also open: rail class and guide angular error (assumed 5"), thermal dT, A torsional stiffness (ZeroErr), spindle bearing/arbor runouts (assumed 2 um each).

## Phase L: MachineKit library

- **L1. Integrity checks** (`AssemblyIntegrity`): minimum running gap between bodies that move relative to
  each other (default 1 mm, configurable) over the sampled sweep; minimum contact area for fixed mates; every
  flanged part fixed to a parent must find its `mountingCutout` in that parent. Unit tests reproduce a rubbing
  face, a 20 x 20 tab and a plate with only a shaft hole, and each fails.
- **L2. Screw drive in `motion/`:** move `MillScrewDrive` out of `milling/`, add placement (axis start,
  direction, mounting side) of bracket, BF12, ring, coupling, servo and nut from catalogue datums, with
  clearance holes and dowel locations. The bench mill keeps its poses.
- **L3. Ball-nut mounting:** `mountingCutout` for the ball nut; a flat-bar nut-block part.
- **L4. Manufacturing route per part:** ground plate and ground flat bar stock rows (standard thicknesses,
  grinding tolerances); each part tagged ground stock / cut plate / milled (setups, tolerance class) /
  turned, with machined faces flagged, so the parts list shows what goes to which supplier.
- **L5. eRob family:** flanges and spigots from the drawing, ratio rows, encoder grades, brake, 48 V. Bearing
  moment rating and brake holding torque marked assumed.
- **L6. Sealed scale family:** housing plus reading head, overall length, mounting tolerances, BiSS-C; JFT-40
  and JFT-10 rows from the datasheets.
- **L7. Servos:** confirm the braked `ELM2H-0400LA60E` row; catalogue servos the default over `GENERIC-*`.
- **L8. Electrical:** 48 V supply able to absorb regenerated energy; BiSS master on the controller.

## Phase P: product round (2026-11-05, Codex)

Decided by the user and lead after reviewing the design in the editor. One commit per step; every step keeps the baselines.

- **P1. Standard BT30 belt-drive spindle.** Replace the assumed Ø80 cartridge (`HobSpindle`/`HobSpindlePart`) with a catalogue row
  for a standard Ø80-housing BT30 belt-driven spindle (reference: RobotDigg "Spindle BT30 80mm with Timing Belt Pulley", HTD5M
  pulleys; Guangzhou Haozhi belt-driven BT30 states ≤3 µm taper and face runout, G0.4). Values not on a public page are labelled
  assumed. Add a BT30 hob arbor part (a BT30 shank, a Ø16 hob seat with key and lock nut, and an outboard journal), and keep the
  B belt drive and the spindle-side encoder on the rear stub. The split-clamp seat follows the real housing.
- **P2. Outboard support as a real bearing housing.** A preloaded bearing pair, or a precision live centre, in a housing that is
  aligned to the spindle axis at assembly (indicated, then dowelled). Add an assembly procedure and a check on the coaxiality
  budget (the outboard bore against the spindle axis).
- **P3. B motor beside the cartridge (study, then decide).** Compare motor-on-top (today) with motor beside the cartridge on the
  Y saddle on these measures: head mass and centre of mass, A torque, the eRob tilt moment, first modes, clearances over A ±15°,
  and parts. Adopt the beside layout only if it is better and passes every check; record the comparison either way.
- **P4. Chip protection, option (b).** Fixed cover plates over the X screw channel (bolted to the bed and inserts, clear of the
  nut-block path), a skirt on the X carriage that overlaps them, and the existing block wipers. The enclosure contains the oil
  mist. Declare drains only where a real path exists; there is no casting change. Do the same for the Z column channel where it
  is exposed.
- **P5. Serviceability.** Move the X nut's flange to the motor side of its nut block, so the X screw and nut withdraw rearward
  through the declared service path (`HobberFrameChecks.service` must show the X screw path clear). Record that the eRob is
  removed with the Z saddle, and declare that path.
- **Y centred screw:** blocked on SFU1204/BK10/BF10/40-frame data; not in this round.
