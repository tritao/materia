# Metrology and replication sourcing

Purchase options for the rail-mounting plan ([RAIL_MOUNTING.md](RAIL_MOUNTING.md)). Prices and
availability are distributor snapshots found on 2026-10-20 and will change; specifications are seller
claims unless a certificate is received. Check every instrument on arrival by reversal (RM-D6).

Import notes for EU delivery: UK orders (Raptor Supplies, MQS) carry import VAT, possible duty and a
courier handling fee since Brexit. US orders add transatlantic freight, which matters for granite.
Chinese orders add freight and import VAT; granite is heavy, so quote shipping before ordering.

## Suppliers at a glance

- **Raptor Supplies** (London; online reseller of US and Japanese brands, ships to 100+ countries):
  useful for Mitutoyo 543-790B indicators, USB-ITN cables, 513-405-10T, magnetic bases, possibly a
  Starrett granite straightedge (81650). Search the part numbers; compare with EU dealers after import
  costs.
- **TAC Rockford** (Rockford, Illinois): German-made granite squares only; buy the same product
  through the maker or an EU dealer.
- **K-MET/KINEX** (EU): granite and steel straightedges, Sylvac indicators.
- **Hoffmann Group** (EU): surface plates, Mitutoyo and MAHR instruments.
- **MQS** (UK): Mitutoyo granite squares, Chotest instruments.
