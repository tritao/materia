Files here
----------
ual-standard.glb  The free Standard glTF (46 clips) as mirrored at
                  https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library, its .gltf and .bin packed into one .glb;
                  nothing else changed.
ual-work.glb      What the runtime loads: ual-standard.glb plus the clips under clips/, built by
                  tools/ual/build_library.py.
clips/            Individual clips of the full library (purchased by the project), one glTF per clip, retargeted onto
                  the free glTF's rig. Where each came from is in clips.json and in PROVENANCE.md.
